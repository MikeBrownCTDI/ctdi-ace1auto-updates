Install for iPhone
  • Open this page in Safari on your iPhone (not Chrome).
  • Tap Install for iPhone, then Install on the prompt.
  • Find the app on your Home Screen while it downloads.
  • First launch: go to Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, tap Communications Test Design, Inc., and tap Trust.

iOS 16 or later

Download for Android
  • Tap Download for Android, then open the downloaded APK.
  • If prompted, allow Install unknown apps for your browser (Settings offers the toggle inline).
  • Tap Install, then Open.
